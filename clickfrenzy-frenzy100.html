What we check
The price

Is it a real saving? Including whether it's cheaper than the competition, and how it stacks up against its own price history.

The product

Is it a popular product? Highly rated by real shoppers, and something people actually want.

The store

A name you can trust. Think free shipping, easy returns and a great reputation.
